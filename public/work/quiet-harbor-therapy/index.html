Your first visit

Before you book.

A free 15 minute intro call gives you time to ask about the practical things. Here are three places you might start.

What would a session cost?

You can ask about the session fee, insurance and whether a lower fee is available. It’s okay to ask for the amount before you decide.

How do we find out if it’s a good fit?

Ask how the therapist usually works and what decisions you would make together. You can take time after the call to think it over.

What happens at the first visit?

You might want to know who you’ll meet, what paperwork comes first, or how privacy is handled. Pick whichever question matters to you.
