Most salespeople know the number. Few have a plan to hit it.

Many equipment sales reps start the year with a target and no written territory plan. They stay busy, they react to whoever calls, and they find out in the fourth quarter that busy did not add up to the number.

Territory Reset™: build the plan behind the number.

A hands on working session. You bring your territory and your target. You leave with a plan in your own workbook, built in six steps.

Ask about the next session
  1. STEP 01

    Evaluate territory potential

    See where the revenue in your territory sits today and where it could come from next.

  2. STEP 02

    Identify high value accounts

    Rank your accounts so the best opportunities get your time first.

  3. STEP 03

    Establish realistic targets

    Break the annual goal into targets by product line and account that you can defend.

  4. STEP 04

    Build your territory plan

    Put it together in the Territory Reset™ Excel planning workbook, with your own accounts and numbers.

  5. STEP 05

    Set accountability objectives

    Turn the plan into weekly activity targets you and your manager can track.

  6. STEP 06

    Create follow up processes

    Leave with a 90 day action plan, so the work starts the week after the session.

Questions before you book.

Something else on your mind? Ask Tom directly.

Ask a question
How long is a Territory Reset™ session?

The live workshop runs about 90 minutes. Private sessions for a dealership or a dealer meeting are planned around your agenda.

Is it online or in person?

The workshop runs live online. Private training can come to your dealership, your branch or your dealer meeting.

What should I bring?

Your annual sales target and your account list. You work on your own territory, not a case study. Tom sends the planning workbook before the session.

What do I take home?

Your completed Territory Reset™ workbook: revenue targets by product line, ranked accounts, the pipeline your number needs, weekly activity targets and a 90 day action plan.

Who is it for?

Equipment sales reps, sales managers and dealer principals. It works for one rep, a full dealership team or a whole dealer network.

What does it cost?

It depends on the format and the size of your group. Book a call and Tom will put together options for your team.

What happens after the session?

You start your 90 day plan the next week. Coaching and accountability follow up is available if you want help keeping the plan on track.
