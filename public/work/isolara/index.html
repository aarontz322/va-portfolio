Sample itinerary

Three nights on the coast.

The villa is your base. Open a day to see where a harbor lunch, a coastal drive, or an afternoon by the pool could fit.

Length of stay
Use this itinerary
Day 1Settle into the villa

A driver meets you on arrival and takes the coastal road to the villa. Leave your bags, walk down to the terrace, and take your first swim.

Evening Stay close to home with a simple supper by the water.

Day 2Follow the coastal road

Take a short drive to a harbor for coffee and a waterfront walk. Plan one lunch stop and leave the return route open for a viewpoint along the way.

Afternoon Return to the pool or ask the driver to linger at the harbor.

Day 3Save the evening for dinner

Keep the morning free for the terrace. Later, leave enough time for a stop above the bay on the way to dinner.

At the table Tell your advisor what you enjoy eating and whether you prefer a quiet corner or a lively room.

Day 4One last morning by the sea

Match breakfast to your departure time. For a later flight, leave the morning open for a swim before your driver arrives.

Departure Build in time for the coastal road and your onward connection.
