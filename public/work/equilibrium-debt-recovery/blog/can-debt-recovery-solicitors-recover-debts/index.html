When to involve a debt recovery solicitor

Recognise the decisions that need legal advice and prepare a focused instruction.

Identify the legal question

A disputed contract term, a threatened counterclaim or an approaching deadline can change the next step. Write down the specific decision you need help with and the date by which you need it.

Give the adviser a complete picture

Provide the contract, invoice, correspondence and evidence of delivery. Include material that supports the customer’s position as well as your own. A short chronology helps a solicitor find gaps without reconstructing the whole file.

Agree the scope in writing

Ask who will carry out the work, what their fee estimate covers and how you will authorise further stages. A settlement letter, advice on prospects and issuing proceedings are separate pieces of work.
