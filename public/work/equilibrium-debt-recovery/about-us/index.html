About Equilibrium

Alan Mills founded Equilibrium in 2019 to bring the experience of a credit manager to businesses chasing unpaid accounts.

Why Equilibrium?

An invoice can sit unpaid while two businesses still need to work together. That relationship shapes how we approach recovery.

A specialist on your account

We ask what has already been said and what matters to you as a supplier. Your specialist can then follow up with the customer and bring payment proposals back to you.

Rooted in the UK

Our offices are in London and Essex, and we work with businesses across the UK. The location of the parties and the terms of the agreement help determine the recovery route.
