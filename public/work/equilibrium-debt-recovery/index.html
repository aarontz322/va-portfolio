What happens after the first call?

We check the invoice, listen to your account of the conversation and agree how to approach your customer.

  1. Tell us what’s outstanding

    We review the amount owed, your paperwork and what your customer has said so far.

  2. Agree a recovery plan

    Your specialist explains the options, the likely costs and how we’ll approach your customer. You decide whether to proceed.

  3. Hear how it’s progressing

    We follow up, negotiate and report back. You decide how to respond to settlement offers or a proposed escalation.
