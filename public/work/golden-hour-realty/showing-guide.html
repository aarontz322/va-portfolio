5 questions listing photos can’t answer.

A house feels different in person. Take five questions to your next showing and write down what you notice while you’re there.

The five questions.

Go to my field sheet
01Street noiseCan I hear traffic from inside?

Listen with a window open and then shut it. Notice what you can hear from the room where you would sleep or work.

02Furniture fitWill my couch fit through the door?

Measure your couch at home. At the showing, ask to measure the narrowest doorway and check for tight turns between the entry and the living room.

03RainwaterDoes water pool near the house?

From ground level, look at where the downspouts empty. Ask where puddles form after a storm.

04Heating and coolingWhen was the AC last serviced?

Ask for the furnace and AC service records. Note when each unit was installed and when it was last checked.

05AppliancesDoes the fridge stay?

Ask about the washer and dryer, too. Request a written list of the appliances included with the home.
