How we work
with buyers.

  1. Start with the way you live.

    Tell us which rooms you spend time in, what needs to fit, and which parts of your day a move should make easier. We use that list to frame the search.

  2. Look closely at a short list.

    Compare the layout, the approach to the front door, and the setting outside. Take the same questions to each showing so the answers are easy to compare later.

  3. Keep the next questions together.

    After the tour, record what you noticed and what you still need to confirm. The showing field sheet keeps each home’s notes in one place.
